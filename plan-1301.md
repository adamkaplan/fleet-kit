## Cause (clause 1)

The test-cloud job of .github/workflows/test.yml has only checkout, python version, bin/test-cloud-worker and bin/test-cloud-launcher (last step). No step runs any of the seven suites, so the hub and the pod-side command are untested in CI. Measured on the integration tip, one at a time: hub 90 s (92 tests), channel under 1 s, channel-server 40 s, client under 1 s (42 tests), way-back 1 s, decisions-pull 72 s, cli 60 s (37 tests); all exit 0; none needs docker, a cluster or the network. Four end with "RESULT: PASS" (channel, channel-server, way-back, decisions-pull); three are plain unittest.main() suites with no such line (hub, client, cli), which the existing step form (tee, then grep -qx 'RESULT: PASS') would fail.

## Pinned test edited

bin/test-workflow-split pins the job: CLOUD_STEPS (around line 174) lists the test-cloud steps exactly and in order, and NEW_JOB (around line 122) lists the suite steps of the old job. Both are edited to add the seven steps. Other step-counting there (grep 'step'): the step-list equality test (test_steps_both_jobs_have_exactly_the_expected_step_lists), the per-step key-class mutants over STEP_LISTS, and the extra-step-at-start-or-end mutant; they derive from CLOUD_STEPS and follow the edit. A pinned test of an earlier ask is edited here because the plan names it.

## Open choices (one recommendation each)

- (a) The three suites without the marker (hub, client, cli). Recommendation: end each with unittest.main(exit=False), then print "RESULT: PASS" if the result wasOk, else exit nonzero; exit codes kept. Every step then checks the marker the same way, and a failing suite fails its step. Cost: three small suite edits.
- (b) Timeouts. Recommendation: timeout-minutes 5 on hub, decisions-pull and cli; none on the fast ones.
- (c) Placement. Recommendation: after bin/test-cloud-launcher, in the order hub, channel, channel-server, client, way-back, decisions-pull, cli.
- (d) macOS. Recommendation: both legs (the suites use only Python).
- (e) Exactly-one proof. Recommendation: extend bin/test-workflow-split with a check parsing the workflow and asserting each of the seven names appears in exactly one test-cloud step, plus a mutant (a workflow copy with one step removed fails it).

## Out of scope

Any other workflow step or job, the suites' test content, the launcher, docker or cluster tests.
