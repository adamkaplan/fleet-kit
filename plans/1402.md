## Plan (scenarios)

plan-format: 2
size: M (one delivery-time step in the deliver path, a sent-time prefix in `compose`, a staleness check reusing #1378's read, a digest replace rule, tests with three mutants, a docs section; a few hours of agent time)
unchanged: the once-per-key memory of alerts (#1378), the ordering and cap of `compose`, acknowledgement of carried keys, and the digest cadence gate, because only what is shown and dropped at delivery changes

### Cause (clause 1, stated first)
Facts are made at one time and delivered at another; nothing between the two looks at the gap or at whether the fact is still true.
- Where a fact is made: the unowned alert line is built by `unowned_line` in `bin/red_ci.py`; the alert-label line by `alert_line` in `bin/switchboard/alert_labels.py` (`alert_wake_pass` keeps the fact with its creation time `at`, kept for `ALERT_FACT_KEEP`); the triage digest is a sweep/triage wake fact (`bin/triage_digest.py` gates how often one is made).
- Where the delay arises: a fact is queued as a pending fact for its recipient. `decide` (in `bin/switchboard/decision.py`) holds it while the recipient is busy, idle-batched or unreachable (the hold and batch rules), and the daemon retries on later passes. The fact keeps its old `created` time the whole while. So an alert sent at 17:16Z and held for an idle Chief of Staff queue is delivered the next morning, and hours-old digests wait in the same queue.
- Where it is delivered with no check: `deliver` (`bin/fleet-switchboard`, about line 2961) calls `merge_wake_facts(plan.recipient, plan.pending)` (`bin/switchboard/reminders.py`, which sorts by `created` and merges sweep, triage and load items) and then `compose(...)`. `compose` writes "(sent X, delivered Y)" into the header only when `delivered` is given and only as a range, never first in a line, never marked late. The merged wake fact takes `max(f.created)` so an old digest looks new. Nothing re-reads the subject: a red check on an old head, a closed issue, a standing line already posted are all delivered as they were made.
- What #1378 covers: only the duplicate, stale-head and closed-PR alert (once per pull request, head and check, never for a stale head or closed PR). It does NOT cover the delivery lag: an alert that is first and unique, on the current head, still arrives hours late and unmarked, and it does not cover issue-closed alerts, standing lines or digests.
- Recorded cases of 26-27 Oct: an alert sent at 17:16Z delivered the next morning; hours-old digests delivered as fresh.

### Approach (what, not how)
At delivery (not at creation), each alert or digest fact is judged once: (a) its sent time is shown first in its line; (b) if older than 30 minutes the first line says "delayed: sent HH:MMZ"; (c) an alert whose subject is no longer true is dropped and audited, using the same present-state read #1378 uses for the stale-head and closed-PR check (the pull request's current head and open state read at the moment of delivery; the plan reuses that read, it adds no second one), extended to an issue's open state and a standing line's posted state by the same read; (d) a digest older than 6 hours is replaced by the current digest and the old one is dropped with an audit line. A dropped fact is still acknowledged so it does not return. A read that fails delivers the fact marked delayed and unverified, never silently drops it. All checks use a scratch state only (never the live state directory).

### Done when (copied exactly from the ask)
Done when: (1) the cause is found and stated first from the recorded cases of 26-27 Oct (an alert sent at 17:16Z was delivered the next morning; hours-old digests arrived as fresh): where the delay arises (a held or idle recipient queue, the daemon's retry) and that #1378 covers only the duplicate and stale-head alert, not the delivery lag; (2) every delivered alert and digest carries its sent time first, and one older than 30 minutes at delivery is marked "delayed: sent HH:MMZ" in its first line; (3) an alert whose subject is no longer true at delivery (a red check on a head that is no longer the head, an issue already closed, a standing line already posted) is dropped and audited, by the same present-state read #1378 uses; (4) a digest older than 6 hours is replaced by the current one and the old one is dropped with an audit line; (5) tests with mutants (no sent time; a stale alert delivered; an old digest kept) and the documentation says it.

### Work item 1: late and stale alerts and digests at delivery
Done when: as above, clauses (1) to (5).

### Out of scope
The once-per-key memory and its keys (#1378); the recipient's hold, batch and retry rules themselves (the lag is made visible, not removed); new GitHub writes; the digest's cadence gate; messages of kind send or report from agents.

### Areas touched
`deliver` and `compose` path (`bin/fleet-switchboard`, `bin/switchboard/decision.py`, `bin/switchboard/reminders.py`), `bin/red_ci.py` and `bin/switchboard/alert_labels.py` (sent time on the line), `docs/switchboard.md`, `bin/test-switchboard`. The daemon file is a held-PR area: the floor decides the hold.

### Risks and rollback
Risk: dropping a true alert if the present-state read is stale; a failed read delivers marked unverified instead (S4). Risk: clock skew gives a wrong delay; the time is the fact's own `created`. Rollback: revert the pull request; the dropped facts are in the audit log.
