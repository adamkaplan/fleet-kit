### Cause (clause 1, stated first)
In `compose` (`bin/fleet-switchboard`, about line 3576) the facts are packed by `for count in range(len(facts), 0, -1): text = render(count); if len(text) <= cap: return text, facts[:count]`. When even one fact does not fit, the loop ends and the last lines (about 3636) run: `marker = "\n[cut at %d characters]" % cap`, then `return text[:cap - len(marker)] + marker, facts[:1]`. So a single unmerged sweep or triage fact (built by `sweep_queue_fact`, about line 12761, and the triage digest facts, about line 14439) whose text exceeds `MESSAGE_CAP` (4000, line 3282) is cut with "[cut at 4000 characters]" and the fact is returned as carried, so the caller derives `keys = [fact.key for fact in carried]` and acknowledges that key: the cut part is never delivered, and nothing says so but the marker. The too-long refusal in the delivery path (about line 4860, `if sent is not None and "\n[cut at ..." in text[-40:]` raising `MessageTooLong`) tests `fact.kind == "send"` only, so it covers sends and never sweep or triage findings. When the cut leaves no member line at all, the same return still reports `facts[:1]`, so the key is acknowledged and the message id (`derive_message_id(observation.session_id, keys)`) is derived from a fact of which nothing was delivered. Shapes, neutral numbers: one sweep fact of 6000 characters arrives as 3980 characters plus the marker and its key is acknowledged; two short facts pack as before.

### Approach (what, not how)
A single finding over the cap is never cut silently. Either its builder shortens its own text to fit (one line per item, the rest named as a count), or the finding is carried over successive messages, each holding a part of its text, and the cut marker never appears on a finding. The key of a finding is acknowledged only when all of its text was delivered; a part delivered leaves the key pending with the remainder. When a cut leaves no member line, nothing is acknowledged, and the message id is derived from the facts carried (never from a fact of which nothing was sent). Sends keep the existing refusal. No change to the channel's other behaviour.
